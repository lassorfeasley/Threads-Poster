House rules

Things that must always hold. Every rule that's switched on is followed, every time. Switch one off to pause it without losing it.

How rules work

Everything rules shape which videos are picked, how clips are cut and what captions say — your stance belongs here. Clips rules only shape which moments are picked and how they're cut. Captions rules only shape what gets written.

A clip that challenges your rules is never presented as credible, and is flagged so you can check how it's framed. When you save, new and edited rules are tidied up — spelling and grammar fixed, your meaning kept.
