Left at zero, the first comment goes out seconds after the post — which puts it in the slot the audience's own first reply would have taken. Hold it back until the post has a conversation of its own, then let it land under that.

Minutes, replies, likes, hours. The comment goes out once the wait has passed and the post has that many replies from other people and that many likes — or at the deadline, whichever comes first. Set the deadline to 0 to wait indefinitely instead. You can always post it by hand from the post page.

The deadline only ever fires on posts that flopped — anything that got its replies was commented under hours earlier. So left unqualified it attaches the comment to your weakest posts and only your weakest posts. These set a floor: at the deadline the comment goes out only if the post cleared either number, and a post under both is passed over for good rather than left carrying a pitch nobody will read. Both at 0 = no floor.
