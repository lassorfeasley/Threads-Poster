How this is used

Enabled rules go into the caption-drafting prompt as a menu of moves you like (High-priority first), on top of the voice the AI already learns from your published captions. It chooses the single rule that fits the clip — stacking several is what makes a caption read like a template.

Hard rules always hold regardless of what you write: the AI won't invent facts that aren't in the clip's transcript, and length outranks every rule here — a move that costs an extra sentence gets skipped rather than stretching the caption.

Watch the length cost of your own rules. Anything phrased as “…then explain it” or “end with a question” mandates a second sentence, and a few of those enabled together are what produce a long, template-shaped caption. If drafts are coming back too wordy, turning rules off works better than adding more.
