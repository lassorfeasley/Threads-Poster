How captions are written

The rules you switch on go to the caption writer as a menu of moves you like, with Try first rules at the top. It also learns your voice from the captions you've posted. It picks the single rule that suits the clip — stacking several is what makes captions read like a template.

Two things always hold, whatever the rules say: it never adds facts that aren't in the clip, and it keeps to the length target (at most {{ max_chars }} characters). A rule that would need an extra sentence gets skipped instead.

If drafts come back too wordy, switching rules off works better than adding more — rules like “…then explain it” or “end with a question” each add a sentence.
