This policy explains what information Continuity Marketing ("we", "us") collects when you visit continuity.marketing or use Throughline, our service that publishes posts to your social media accounts, and what we do with it.

We are [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], doing business as Continuity Marketing. Questions about this policy go to [hello@continuity.marketing](mailto:hello@continuity.marketing).

# What we collect

**When you contact us.** If you fill in the form on our website, we receive your name, work email, organization and website, and we use them to reply to you.

**When you have an account.** Your name, email address and password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form), and the settings you give us: your organization's topics, voice, rules, posting schedule and email preferences.

**When you connect a social account.** When you connect Threads, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn or X, that platform gives us an access token that lets Throughline publish on your behalf. Depending on the platform, we can also read your profile name, the posts we publish, their view and engagement counts, and replies to those posts. We only request the permissions needed to publish, read performance and manage replies to our posts. We never see your social media passwords.

**Content we work with.** The videos, captions and posts we make for you, including excerpts of publicly available videos and any files you upload to us.

**Technical information.** When you sign in, we set one cookie to keep you signed in. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and time of request) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on our website.

# How we use it

- To run the service: find and make posts for you, publish them on the accounts you connected, and report how they perform.
- To improve your posts over time, based on your feedback and on how past posts performed.
- To email you about your account, such as clips waiting for review and account notices. You can change how often we email you in your settings.
- To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

# Artificial intelligence

Throughline uses AI models to choose, edit and caption posts. To do this, we send the relevant text (such as video transcripts, captions and your account's topics and rules) to our AI provider, Anthropic. Under our agreement with them, they do not use this data to train their models. Speech transcription runs on our own systems.

# Who we share it with

We share information only with the providers that run the service for us, and only what each one needs:

- **Supabase** (database, file storage and sign-in)
- **Fly.io** and **Vercel** (hosting)
- **Anthropic** (AI processing)
- **Resend** (email delivery)
- **The social platforms you connect** (Meta for Threads, Instagram and Facebook; LinkedIn; X), which receive the posts we publish for you

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. If Continuity Marketing is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

# How long we keep it

We keep your account information and posts while your account is open. When you close your account, we revoke our access to your connected social accounts and delete your information within [NUMBER] days, except where we need to keep records for legal or accounting reasons. Posts already published stay on your social accounts unless you remove them.

# Your choices and rights

- **Disconnect a social account** at any time from your settings or from the platform's own settings. We stop publishing and reading from it right away.
- **See, correct or delete your information** by emailing [hello@continuity.marketing](mailto:hello@continuity.marketing). We will respond within 30 days.
- **Delete data received from Meta.** To have us delete the information we received through Threads, Instagram or Facebook, remove Throughline from your connected apps in that platform's settings, or email us asking for deletion. We will confirm when it is done.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

# Security

We encrypt information in transit, limit who on our team can access it, and keep access tokens out of reach of the public. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you promptly if a breach affects it.

# Children

Throughline is a service for organizations. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

When we change this policy, we publish a new version here with its effective date. Earlier versions remain available below. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
